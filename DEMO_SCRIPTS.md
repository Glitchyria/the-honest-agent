# Demo Scripts

## DEMO 1 — Wired Earphones

Type: `Looking for wired earphones under ₹2000 for daily commute`

Expected flow:
- Shows only wired options.
- Click Sony MDR-XB55AP to open the full product page.
- Click the AI Honest Analysis tab.
- Show Review Autopsy, Fake Review Detector, Regret Risk, and alternatives.
- Click Get Honest Verdict.
- Add to Cart and proceed to Checkout Guardian.

## DEMO 2 — Low Review Product

Open Floral Wrap Dress.
